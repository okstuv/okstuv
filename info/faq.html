FAQ

Is it really just "OK"?

We strive for mediocrity and occasionally fail upward into "adequate." Anything more would be showing off.

Where can I buy your products?

Mostly in the lost-and-found bins of various Pacific Northwest trailheads, or occasionally on eBay if we remember our password.

How do I join OK Stuv?

First, you must successfully assemble a piece of furniture without looking at the instructions, then intentionally leave one screw out. Then you'll know the true meaning of OK.

Do you offer a warranty?

Our warranty lasts exactly as long as it takes for the check to clear, or until the next squirrel distracts us. Whichever comes first.

Why "Stuv" with a 'v'?

Because the 'ff' keys on our 1990s typewriter were sticky, and we've been too committed to the bit to change it since.
