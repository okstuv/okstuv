OK Stuv

OK Stuv is a group of people who do and make stuff. OK Stuv was founded (?) in the 1990s when we needed a good name for the stuff we did. We weren't aiming for perfection, it was just OK stuff, so we called it OK Stuv.

What do we make? Outdoor stuv (Mancala, Crampons, snowshoes, quinzhee huts, debris huts), videography (Bend Film Festival entries, Yikes Bikes), gadgets (homemade drones, cross-bows, homemade sawmill feat. VW minibus flywheel pulleys, slime), and commercial products (OK Stuv BMW e30 Keyless Entry Kits — installation manual; Greenworks weedwacker bump knob (eBay)). Services: Phantom 2 Vision drone repair.
